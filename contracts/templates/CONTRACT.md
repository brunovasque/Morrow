## 10. Preflight status

- discovery complete: `yes | no`
- diagnostic evidence complete: `yes | no | not-applicable`
- multi-role question round complete: `yes | no`
- blocking questions open: `<count>`
- execution map reviewed: `yes | no`
- regression baseline identified: `yes | no | not-applicable`
- state-of-art scan required: `yes | no`
- state-of-art scan complete: `yes | no | not-applicable`
- ready for execution: `yes | no`

`ready for execution: yes` is not an LLM opinion. It is the output of the deterministic CONTRACT_PREFLIGHT gate.
